🚫

No Data Collection

WidgetScale does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All operations are performed entirely locally on your device and are never sent to any external server.

📵

No Ads or Tracking

This application contains no advertisements, no tracking networks, and no behavioral analytics of any kind. Your experience is 100% clean.

🌐

No Internet Access

WidgetScale does not require or use internet access. All features work completely offline. The app is designed to be an offline-first tool.

🔒

Required Permissions

This application requests no special device permissions. We do not need access to camera, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device resources.

💾

Local Storage

The app stores only your simple preferences (chosen theme and preferred language) locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy in the future, we will reflect those changes in new app versions and update the "Last updated" date above.
